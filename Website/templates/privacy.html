{{t:"Reminders use local notifications with your permission. Face ID, Touch ID and device passcode authentication are handled by the operating system. Routine does not receive biometric templates. You can manage permissions in system settings."}}

{{t:"Routine includes no developer analytics, advertising trackers or third-party crash reporting. The app and operating system may produce local diagnostic logs."}}

{{t:"If you choose to email support, the app’s draft includes the date, device model, operating system version and app version. Review or remove these details before sending. {{t:"Deleting data"}}

{{t:"On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app normally removes app-local data. On Mac, removing the app can leave its local app-container and shared App Group data behind. CloudKit data and iCloud backups may remain under your Apple account and need separate management through Apple’s iCloud storage settings. Deleting the app does not guarantee deletion of every copy. Contact support for help identifying stored Routine data."}}
