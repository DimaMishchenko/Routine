Existing subscription transition

Routine’s subscription products were removed from sale on 28 October 2026 to stop new signups and renewals as part of the transition to a free app.

If you use the older App Store version, 1.0.4, and have a subscription or paid-access issue, contact dimasike.dev@gmail.com. Include your app version and a description of the access issue. Individual paid-access questions still need to be reviewed.

Apple handles refund requests and decides eligibility. To ask Apple to review a purchase or subscription, use reportaproblem.apple.com. See Apple’s refund request instructions. A refund is not automatic or guaranteed.

Will all features be free in the update?

Routine 1.1.0 is being prepared without subscriptions, in-app purchases or paywalls. It has not shipped yet. The older App Store version, 1.0.4, may have different behavior.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check that reminders are enabled for the routine and notifications are allowed for Routine in system settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect how reminders appear.

Where are my routines stored?

Your routines are stored on your device. Official versions with iCloud support can also sync through your Apple account. A local-only version does not provide iCloud synchronization.

The app cannot open its data

Use the Retry button if the app shows a storage error. Contact support if the problem continues. Avoid deleting the app while investigating: deletion removes the app’s local storage.
