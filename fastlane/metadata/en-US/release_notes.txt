Routine is now fully free. All features are available without subscriptions or in-app purchases, and third-party analytics have been removed.

• A simpler interface with Settings and a prominent Add button in the toolbar.
• A one-time welcome to the free app, with links to its authors.
• Ten new interface languages, alongside English and Ukrainian.
• Improved calendar, editing, keyboard and large-text layouts.
• Library acknowledgements and updated author/support information.
• More reliable iCloud status reporting.

Your existing routines and history are preserved. This version requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or macOS 26 or later.
