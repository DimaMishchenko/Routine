Build routines at your own pace. Routine helps you track daily, weekly and monthly goals, record progress and see your history in a calendar and charts.

All features are free:
• Create unlimited routines and customize goals.
• Schedule local reminders.
• Protect the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
• Sync routines across your Apple devices with iCloud.
• Use light or dark appearance on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

No subscriptions, in-app purchases, app account or third-party analytics. Routine is open source.
