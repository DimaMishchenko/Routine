Build habits at your own pace. Routine helps you track daily, weekly and monthly goals, record progress, and explore your history in a calendar and charts.

Every feature is available for free:
• Create unlimited routines with an emoji, description and a goal that fits your day.
• Track progress and undo a completion when you need to.
• Schedule optional local reminders.
• Explore your routine history and progress insights.
• Protect the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
• Sync across your Apple devices through your own iCloud account.
• Use a native interface on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with light and dark appearance.

No subscriptions, in-app purchases, separate Routine account or third-party analytics.

Routine is open source under the MIT license. Explore the code on GitHub:
https://github.com/DimaMishchenko/Routine

Routine is in maintenance mode, with a focus on essential fixes and compatibility.

Requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or macOS 26 or later. Supports English, Ukrainian, Simplified Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Bengali, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian, German and Japanese.
